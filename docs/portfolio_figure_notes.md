## Deliverables and interpretation

- `figure_1_trajectories`: three selected localities, recorded quarterly events and held-out history predictions alongside monthly listed stocks. Orange stock markers identify actual source-map months; monthly stock steps between markers are carried forward. The model uses prior-quarter mean strength, not contemporaneous stock. Panels have independent vertical scales.
- `figure_1_all_localities`: all nine observed-event and held-out prediction trajectories, with independent count scales. Prediction bands are conditional plug-in 95% NB2 count intervals from expanding windows, not parameter-inclusive forecast uncertainty.
- `figure_2_simulation_recovery`: average estimated coefficients for ordinary-model synthetic scenarios. Negative-effect and null truths are separated. Bars quantify Monte Carlo uncertainty in the estimated mean, while labels report coverage of the individual model-based coefficient intervals. These are different uncertainty quantities.
- `figure_3_reporting_sensitivity`: the exact algebraic shift of the supported-correction coefficient under assumed log-linear recording probabilities. The ribbon is conditional coefficient uncertainty, not uncertainty about the recording mechanism. The corrected interval already includes zero. No new offset fits are claimed.
- `figure_4_empirical_sensitivity`: the actual common coefficient and conditional Wald intervals for the original panel, supported corrections, and Mavivi 2,350 alternative. All retain nine localities, 288 quarters and 1,248 recorded events. It is not a distribution of locality effects.
- `figure_5_prediction_comparison`: alphabetical locality mean log-score differences, plus the aggregate mean. This compares forecasting procedures, not protection outcomes; it is not a locality-success ranking.

Memo-sized versions of figures 2 and 3 are exported at 6.5 by 2.8 inches. Full versions provide more caption space. Scientific PNG and PDF figures are intentional public portfolio outputs; chart-ready aggregate summaries accompany them. Source-row and locality-quarter plotting tables remain in ignored `outputs/portfolio_figures/`. No raw maps, source files, or event records are redistributed.

Sources are the existing audited panel, held-out prediction records, deployment lineage and source-row FPU fields, dynamic simulation summaries, and empirical sensitivity aggregates. The companion R script contains paths, assertions and all transformations. Every visual is checked against the saved results and inspected after export.

The corrected figures additionally read `data/corrected_panel_model_comparison.csv` and the supported-correction panel. The corrected coefficient is -0.188535 (95% Wald interval -0.829557 to 0.452487), compared with -0.132590 in the original panel and -0.210155 in the Mavivi alternative. All intervals include zero; this does not establish equivalence. Mavivi's analysis subtotal is 2,200, with 2,350 tested only for map revision 51; the provider's original field discrepancy remains unexplained. Nyamilima geography and supported military-component corrections are incorporated, but unknown deployment is not converted to absence and historical information availability is not certified.

For the updated reporting curve, `lambda = log(relative_recording_per_1000)` and each coefficient bound is translated by `-lambda`. Feasible illustrative probabilities are constructed over the corrected observed strengths as `log(q) = log(0.8) + lambda * strength_lag - max(lambda * strength_lag)`. Thus all probabilities lie in (0, 0.8]; these are assumed scenarios, not estimated recording rates. The point estimate crosses zero at a relative ratio of 0.828172. Its conditional interval includes zero for ratios from 0.436243 to 1.572217. These thresholds do not resolve deployment confounding, serial dependence or uncertainty in the reporting assumption itself.
