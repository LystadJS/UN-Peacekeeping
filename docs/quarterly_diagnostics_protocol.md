# Quarterly residual and influence evaluation protocol

Recorded 2026-11-06 before the new diagnostic simulations and deletion fits. Existing empirical coefficients have already been inspected; this is a prospective evaluation addendum, not a claim of a fully preregistered empirical analysis. No model selection or coefficient-driven specification revision is performed here.

Use both saved baseline and ordinary-history models on the same 288 rows. Diagnose NB2 distributions conditional on fitted locality effects and observed regressors. Generate 2,000 independent conditional count replicates with seed 2026110601. Do not use `simulate.glmmTMB`, whose default also simulates random effects; fixed fitted means and NB2 size make the conditioning explicit. Histories remain observed and frozen, so these are one-step distribution checks, not simulated longitudinal trajectories or prospective predictive validation. Compare zero fraction, count total, maximum, empirical 95th percentile (R type 8), and number of quarters with at least 20 recorded events against 95% simulation envelopes.

Randomized quantile residuals use U uniform between F(y−1) and F(y), followed by the standard-normal quantile. Repeat the observed randomization 200 times to show jitter sensitivity. For each draw, calculate pooled same-locality residual correlations at lags 1, 2, and 4 quarters, plus locality-specific lag-1 correlations. Compare the pooled correlations against the same statistics from the conditional count replicates. No adjacent rows from different localities are paired.

For a spatial descriptive check, reconstruct the audited SALB 2018 city–territory composites and take their centroids in EPSG:6933. Connect each of the nine retained localities to its two nearest centroids, symmetrize the binary weights, and use zero diagonal. This fixed, prespecified neighborhood connects measured localities across omitted areas; it is not evidence about every neighboring administrative unit. Calculate Moran's I separately each quarter and average the 32 values. Compare repeated observed-residual randomizations with the conditional simulation envelope. Also retain the spatial weights and quarter-specific statistics locally. Estimated parameters, fitted random effects, and reused outcomes mean these reference envelopes are diagnostic, not calibrated hypothesis tests. Do not report a nominal diagnostic p-value or claim a passed global goodness-of-fit test.

Refit both formulas deleting each of nine localities and each of eight years (34 attempts). Retain original exposure centering to preserve the defined within-strength contrast; retain original preperiod histories and calendar index, including histories adjoining an omitted year. These are case-deletion influence checks, not forecasts or reconstructed counterfactual histories. Drop unused factor levels; deletion of the sole Ituri locality necessarily removes that province comparison. Retain failures and warnings. Summarize the history strength coefficient, Wald interval, change from full-data estimate and change divided by the full-data standard error. This standardized change is descriptive, not a DFBETAS threshold or calibrated outlier test. No deletion is made permanent because of its result.

Sources for software behavior: [R negative-binomial functions](https://stat.ethz.ch/R-manual/R-patched/library/stats/html/NegBinomial.html) and [glmmTMB simulation documentation](https://search.r-project.org/CRAN/refmans/glmmTMB/html/simulate.glmmTMB.html). R and package versions and exact seeds accompany the local outputs. Only aggregate diagnostic and influence summaries are tracked; raw geometry and row-level residuals remain local.

After inspecting the primary randomized-residual results, an auxiliary deterministic Pearson-residual lag-1/2/4 correlation was added to distinguish jitter sensitivity; it is exploratory and has no calibrated reference test. Primary thresholds, models, weights, replicate counts and results were unchanged. Quarter-specific Moran jitter summaries were also exported from the same draws.
