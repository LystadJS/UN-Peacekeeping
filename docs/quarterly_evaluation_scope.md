# Quarterly empirical evaluation scope

Recorded 2026-11-06 in response to the user's request to complete residual diagnostics, influence checks, sensitivity analysis, and predictive validation. This extends the bounded initial fitting task; it does not change the primary sample or authorize causal interpretation.

Three parallel work streams use the existing audited inputs and saved baseline/history fits:

1. Residual and count-distribution diagnostics, temporal and spatial residual dependence, and locality/year deletion influence checks.
2. Assumed reporting-probability sensitivity, deployment timing/freshness alternatives, event inclusion/assignment alternatives, and a separately interpreted fatality outcome. A monthly analogue is exploratory because its exposure/history horizon differs from the validated quarterly model.
3. Expanding-window, one-quarter-ahead retrospective prediction from 2019 Q1 through 2023 Q4. Exposure centering must use training rows only. Both models must be evaluated on the same held-out rows; unsuccessful folds remain in the accounting.

Each stream records its detailed rules before its new calculations in a separate protocol. Follow-up corrections must be disclosed if results expose a coding error or an unsupported interpretation. The original nine-locality sample remains selected using full-period deployment availability; retrospective prediction within it cannot establish prospective eligibility or generalization. GED occurrence dates do not establish historical release availability.

Prediction intervals and log scores based on fitted NB2 parameters and locality effects are conditional plug-in evaluations. Their uncertainty does not include parameter-estimation uncertainty or a newly drawn locality. Diagnostic simulations that fix observed lagged histories are conditional count checks, not simulations of an entire evolving violence process. Neither should be described as a fully calibrated posterior predictive analysis.

The reporting mechanism is assumed, never estimated from these data. Sensitivity to recording does not solve deployment confounding. Unknown deployment remains unknown in every timing scenario; retrospective use of a later source map must be labeled as a measurement scenario and kept out of forecasting predictors.

Completion means reproducible checks, retained failures, figures and documented findings across the requested scope. It does not mean that the model passes every diagnostic or that influential observations, spatial dependence, or measurement uncertainty have been eliminated. Findings will be independently recalculated where possible before the repository update.
