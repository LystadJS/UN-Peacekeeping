# September reporting: the remaining source bottleneck

Investigated 2026-11-07 for the unchanged **1 October 2023, 00:00 UTC** cutoff. The new audit identifies a concentrated provenance gap: **21 of the 23 eligible September Candidate records list only Kivu Security Tracker (KST)**. Five of those records list a pre-cutoff source date; the other 16 list only later material. In the current nine-locality sample, 12 of 13 records are KST-only, including four with earlier listed material and eight with only later material. These are properties of the later retrieved Candidate file, not contemporaneously certified event counts or reporting probabilities. [Generated summary](../data/september_reporting_gap_summary.csv)

## Why retrieving the original incident records remains difficult

The project partners' [9 November 2023 statement](https://www.congoresearchgroup.org/en/2023/11/09/dr-congo-system-vulnerability-of-the-kivu-security-tracker/) reports that they took the KST website offline and paused information collection after discovering a database security vulnerability. This provides a documented explanation for a source-retrieval obstacle; it does not show exactly when individual September records were published, or whether their present UCDP classifications were available before the cutoff. The current public statement was retrieved and hashed. No private system, vulnerability or researcher information was accessed.

ACLED's [own revision notice](https://acleddata.com/update-log/data-update-integration-data-kivu-security-tracker-acleds-democratic-republic-congo) says it incorporated KST material for January 2021–November 2023 on **17 December 2025**. Its current historical records therefore cannot substitute for an authenticated October 2023 information set. ACLED also uses different event definitions from UCDP. The later integration is evidence about ACLED's revision history, not evidence dating UCDP availability or a reason to change this project's outcome.

## What the further searches recovered

Two additional bounded Internet Archive CDX queries returned empty arrays: successful captures through 30 September 2023 of the monthly API route `ucdpapi.pcr.uu.se/api/gedevents/23.0.9*`, and of the named January–September cumulative CSV. The exact queries and retained response hashes are in the [source ledger](../data/september_reporting_gap_sources.csv). Empty results do not establish nonexistence of a release, and do not cover all possible URLs or distribution routes. A broader public KST website capture query failed with a service-unavailable response; that failure is not a negative archive result. No KST incident snapshot was authenticated through this attempt.

A [Radio Elimu report dated 5 September 2023](https://radioelimu.uor.ac/beni-inquietude-de-la-societe-civile-de-mamove-suite-a-la-presence-dangereuse-des-adf-a-mbingi-mapasana-mabuo-et-mabatundu/) describes a recent civilian killing at Mambau attributed to ADF. This is potentially relevant to the KST-only Candidate record for Mambau on 4 September. The article does not establish the exact occurrence day or a unique record match. It remains a current publisher copy bearing an earlier date, not archived contemporaneous bytes. It was preserved as an unresolved corroboration lead, not added as a verified source match or used to recode the event. Bounded searches for the other KST-only earlier-source locations did not yield an authenticated incident-level substitute.

The earlier [download-page evidence](september_2023_availability.md) remains the strongest named-release evidence: August was still advertised on 6 October, while September was advertised by 30 October. Neither this follow-up nor the earlier investigation establishes the exact first September release date. No complete pre-cutoff September coded snapshot was recovered.

## Implication for the historical training set

The seven Candidate records with earlier listed sources remain a retrospective timing proxy. Their provenance comprises five KST-only records, one KST/Bunia Actualité record and one Radio Okapi record. The two earlier publisher checks and the new tentative Mambau lead do not establish an exhaustive source collection, UCDP eligibility, unique coding, or final geographic precision at the origin. **Do not append seven events to the partial July–August total, and do not treat localities without a recovered September record as zero.** Complete September counts and the corresponding full-quarter predictors remain missing.

A defensible source-based alternative would require a separately specified collection window, an exhaustive and reproducible search frame, duplicate reconciliation, actor and event eligibility rules, and source copies with verified availability dates. It would be a different measured information set rather than recovery of the historical GED release. An authenticated provider snapshot would resolve a different and narrower question. Neither alternative has been completed here; no provider was contacted.

## Reproduction and scope

Run `scripts/audit_september_reporting_gaps.R` after `scripts/audit_september_2023.R`. The new script verifies five retained evidence artifacts, checks the two empty query responses, reconstructs the source-office groups, and fingerprints the preceding private Candidate ledger. It produces two public CSVs and one private provenance ledger under ignored `outputs/september_reporting_gaps/`. The source ledger is a static input; HTML and archive responses are retained originals, not assumed to be byte-recoverable from future live requests. All certified historical count fields remain missing. No panel, fitted model, forecast cutoff or human-review disposition changed.
