# Five-slide discussion outline

Prepared for discussion with Marc A. Scott, 6 November 2026. The editable deck is [scott_discussion.pptx](scott_discussion.pptx). Speaker notes contain the fuller interpretation and source links. No meeting or research arrangement is implied.

1. **Protection or apparent protection?** Establish the narrowed question: a common within-locality association between listed strength and subsequent recorded one-sided violence. The audited 2016–2023 sample contains nine of nineteen candidate composites, 288 locality-quarters and 1,248 events. Unverified absence remains unknown; complete exposure relies on maps up to five months old. The outcome is qualifying lethal organized violence, not all civilian harm.
2. **Simulation recovery under known truth.** Compare favorable, endogenous-selection and differential-recording scenarios with a true slope of −0.30 and 200 replications each. Coverage is 95.5%, 68.0% and 81.0%. The two adverse scenarios each introduce about +0.31 mean bias. These illustrative mechanisms do not identify the actual recording process. The dynamic experiment has 1,400 fits on 1,200 generated panels, distinct from the earlier 800-fit core exercise.
3. **An imprecise common deployment association.** The primary conditional count ratio is 0.876 (95% interval 0.463–1.656) per additional 1,000 listed personnel. Early and midpoint map-switch assumptions reverse the point sign while intervals include zero. All seventeen deletion intervals include zero; omitting 2017 materially shifts the estimate. Lag-two residual structure remains. No locality slopes or protection rankings were fitted.
4. **Prediction gains are concentrated.** Across 180 retrospective held-out outcomes, history improves the mean log score by 0.0429, with improvements in fourteen of twenty quarters and four of nine locality averages. Removing Irumu's scores from aggregation reverses the gain; this is not a refit. Training-only centering prevents one identified leakage path, but full-period sample selection, source vintage and plug-in uncertainty limit claims about operational forecasting.
5. **Reporting assumptions and the next study.** Show the algebraic sensitivity curve, conditional on hypothetical recording ratios per 1,000 personnel. It does not estimate reporting probabilities or remove confounding. Propose a bounded comparison of one extra violence-history lag using matched simulation and unchanged measurement rules. Ask Scott whether the added lag is justified, and what evidence should precede locality-specific slopes.
